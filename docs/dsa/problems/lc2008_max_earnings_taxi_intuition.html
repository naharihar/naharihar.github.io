First Principles

How to recognize Taxi Earnings as weighted interval scheduling again

This problem feels new because the intervals are rides and the reward formula includes distance plus tip, but structurally it is the same take-or-skip DP as job scheduling.

LC 2008Pattern 4Weighted rides
Human Version

Every ride is just an interval with a reward

Translation

A ride occupies the interval from pickup to drop-off.

The money earned is fixed once the ride is chosen, so the whole problem is “pick non-overlapping intervals with maximum total reward.”

Brute Force

The direct recursion is identical to job scheduling

For each ride, either skip it or take it and then continue only with rides starting after its end. That reveals the structure immediately, but the repeated subproblems are the same ones we saw in LC 1235.

Optimization

Sort by end point and reuse the weighted scheduling template

Optimization insight The profit formula changes, but the scheduling structure does not. Once rides are sorted by end point, the DP state and binary-search compatibility lookup are exactly the same as weighted job scheduling.

This is a great example of why recognizing the pattern matters more than memorizing the story wrapped around it.

Spot It

When intervals carry money, think weighted scheduling first

Taxi rides, consulting gigs, ad slots, and jobs all reduce to the same shape once each interval has a reward and overlap is forbidden.
